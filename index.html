ABOUT COLLEGE

Welcome to my smart campus

Sahyadri Science College is a constituent government science college affiliated with Kuvempu University in Shivamogga, Karnataka.

Founded in 1940 by Maharaja Jayachamarajendra Wadiyar as an intermediate college.

It offers undergraduate and postgraduate science and computer programs.
